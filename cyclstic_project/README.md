## 🚲 Cyclistic Bike-Share Case Study
> **Google Data Analytics Capstone Project**

---

### 📋 Overview
Analyzed 12 months of historical trip data (2019–2020) to identify how Annual Members and Casual riders use Cyclistic bikes differently, providing data-driven recommendations to increase membership conversions.

### 💡 Key Insights
* **Usage Patterns:** Casual riders bike significantly longer on weekends for leisure, while Annual members have peak usage during weekday commute hours.
* **Recommendations:** Target Casual riders with seasonal weekend-to-annual membership conversion promotions.

## 🔍 Key Insights & Findings

1. *Rides Volume & Usage Patterns:*
   * *Casual Riders:* Peak activity occurs during weekends (Saturday & Sunday), indicating leisure and recreational use.
   * *Annual Members:* Consistent high volume during weekdays (Monday–Friday) with peaks around typical commute hours (8:00 AM & 5:00 PM).

2. *Ride Duration:*
   * Casual riders consistently have longer average ride durations compared to annual members, who tend to take shorter, point-to-point commuting trips.

3. *Bike Preference:*
   * Both groups favor classic bikes, but casual riders utilize electric bikes frequently for longer weekend excursions.

---

## 💡 Top Strategic Recommendations

1. *Targeted Membership Packages:*
   * Introduce flexible membership options, such as a *"Weekend Pass"* or *"Seasonal Membership"*, specifically designed to attract casual weekend users.

2. *Personalized Promotional Campaigns:*
   * Launch digital marketing campaigns targeted at casual riders during peak weekend hours, highlighting the long-term cost savings of an annual subscription.

3. *Incentivize Commuting via Perks:*
   * Offer introductory discounts or loyalty points for casual riders who transition to using bikes during weekday peak commuting hours.
